# Tokenomics

## Overview

The Rustchain token model is designed for sustainability and fairness.

## Supply Distribution

- 40% — Community & Ecosystem
- 25% — Development Fund
- 20% — Team & Advisors (vested)
- 10% — Reserve
- 5% — Liquidity
